# LS-P-009 — MNCS-native capsule admission policy

Status: VERIFIED

## Original pressure (ambient campaign)

Can MNCS express a service-style admission policy over a bounded
finding envelope — relevance classification, per-class budgets,
overflow accounting, and a completeness verdict — executed through
the authoritative compiler on a real backend, with the host reduced
to measurement and mechanical application?

## Resolution

Yes. `mncs/semantic_capsule.mncs`
(`mncs.language_service.semantic_capsule.v1`) implements
`select_capsule` over a fixed 32-finding envelope of
`CapsuleFinding{severity, kind}` records:

- `classify` maps (severity, kind) ranks to relevance ranks
  (actionable / watch / informational) with pure if/else logic;
- the scan loop carries a `CapsuleScan` record with admitted and
  relevance arrays, built incrementally with `replace`;
- budgets are module constants the host cannot widen;
- informational findings are never admitted, only counted;
- the verdict reports counts plus `complete` and `valid` flags.

`crates/service-core/src/ambient.rs` projects measured ranks into
record-valued `ExecutionValue`s, executes the frozen artifact on
`mncs-research-bytecode`, and validates the returned
`CapsuleSelection` shape (including fixed bool/i64 arrays in return
position). There is intentionally no Rust control reimplementation:
withholding on incomplete verdicts plus executed-behavior tests is
the correctness story, and duplicating the decision procedure would
recreate semantic policy in the host against the MNCS-first rule.

## Verification

- `ambient::tests::errors_admit_and_informationals_never_do`:
  classification and admission for all four finding kinds.
- `ambient::tests::budgets_bind_and_overflow_is_counted`:
  per-class/total budgets bind; overflow counted, prefix admitted.
- `ambient::tests::empty_envelope_is_valid_and_complete`.
- `ambient_coherence.rs::capsule_is_bounded_admitted_by_policy_and_expandable`:
  end-to-end capsule over real fixtures with expansion handles.
- No `mncs-language` change was required: profile 0.13 records,
  fixed arrays in argument and return position, `iterate carrying`
  over record state, `replace`, and `select` all worked as
  documented through `front_end_with_resolver` plus the research
  backend.
